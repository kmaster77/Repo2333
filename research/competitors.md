# Competitors & landscape — circular ready-mix / prefab concrete in the Netherlands

Agent 4 (Competitor & landscape analyst). Date: 2026-10-22. Scope: national.
Labels: **[VERIFIED]** = source opened/seen; **[ESTIMATE]** = derived, calculation shown; **[ASSUMPTION]** = no source, reasoning given. Source IDs `CMP-xx` → `sources_competitors.md`. Assumption IDs `A-CMP-xx` → `assumptions_competitors.md`.

Terms used once:
- **RCA** = recycled concrete aggregate (*betongranulaat*): crushed old concrete used instead of gravel/sand.
- **Level 1–5 recycling** (Renewi's scale, CMP-67): 1 = road base only; 2 = recycled gravel in concrete; 3 = recycled gravel + sand; 4 = + filler; 5 = + *reactive* filler (recovered cement that binds again).
- **SCM** = supplementary cementitious material (a powder that replaces part of the cement).
- **MKI** = *milieukostenindicator*, the Dutch environmental cost score (€) used in tenders.
- **Integrated group** = a company that owns cement supply *and* ready-mix plants.

## 1. Key findings (summary)

1. **The best-funded direct competitor raised €100 million three weeks ago.** Urban Mine (Zaandam), the Rutte Groep spin-off that runs the "Smart Liberator", renamed itself **SL Materials** (22 July 2026) and got an initial **€100m commitment from Aviva Investors and ABN AMRO** (6 Oct 2026) to expand Zaandam and build new sites in NL and Europe [VERIFIED, CMP-04, CMP-05]. It already sells "Liberated Concrete" (recycled sand, gravel and cement) and claims up to 90% CO₂ reduction and price parity with conventional concrete [VERIFIED claim, CMP-04, CMP-06].
2. **The second deep-tech competitor, C2CA, is now called "everox"** and is building its first commercial plant in 's-Gravendeel (Hoeksche Waard, Rotterdam region) in a joint venture with contractor **Dura Vermeer**. The plant is permitted for **100,000 t/yr** of demolition concrete. It was planned for Q2 2025 and is still under construction in Sept 2026 [VERIFIED, CMP-16, CMP-17].
3. **Incumbents already sell "circular" and "low-CO₂" ready-mix at a small premium.** Heidelberg Materials (ex-Mebin, ~30 plants) sells **EcoCrete** with 30/50/100% RCA and up to 70% CO₂ cut [VERIFIED, CMP-28, CMP-29]. Holcim's 2026 public price list shows its **ECOPact** low-CO₂ mix at only **+€5 to +€9 per m³** over standard, and it even charges **+€4/m³ extra** for using up to 30% recycled granulate [VERIFIED, CMP-35].
4. **Recycled aggregate alone gives only ~10% CO₂ reduction; the binder gives the big cut.** EPD data show RCA cuts GWP by 8–12% (model base −10%, A-CMP-05); changing the binder cuts 21–68% [VERIFIED, CMP-27, CMP-29, CMP-41]. Dutch ready-mix is already low-clinker: Mebin uses CEM III/B (blast-furnace cement) in ~85% of products [VERIFIED, CMP-27]. So "circular" is not automatically "much lower CO₂" than the Dutch baseline.
5. **The real gap is level 3–5 recycling (recycled sand and cement fines).** About 95% of Dutch concrete recycling is still level 2 (gravel only) [VERIFIED claim, CMP-67]. Only a handful of players work at level 3+ (SL Materials, Renewi, Circulair Mineraal, Twee "R", everox soon) [VERIFIED, CMP-04, CMP-67, CMP-75, CMP-76, CMP-17].
6. **The market is consolidating and shrinking.** Dutch ready-mix output fell from 7.5 Mm³ (2022) to **6.0 Mm³ (2023)** at **178 plants** (avg. 33,700 m³/plant) [VERIFIED, CMP-43]. CRH/Cementbouw sold its ready-mix and recycling arms (2022–23) [VERIFIED, CMP-38]; Heidelberg bought Kalle en Bakker's Maastricht customers (May 2026) [VERIFIED, CMP-33].
7. **Big barriers:** cement supply is concentrated (ENCI/Heidelberg had 50–60% of NL cement in the last public ACM figure) [VERIFIED, old, CMP-32]; clean rubble is controlled by demolition firms; incumbents have ~178 permitted plant sites. **Best entry route: partner or license, not fight head-on** (see §8–9).

## 10. Gaps in the market a new entrant could exploit

1. **Level 3–5 recycling outside Amsterdam and Rotterdam.** 95% of the market is level 2 [VERIFIED claim, CMP-67]. Level-3+ capacity is in Zaandam (SL Materials), Amsterdam Westpoort (Renewi sand), Stuifzand/Drenthe (Circulair Mineraal), East/North (Twee "R") and soon 's-Gravendeel (everox). The centre and south (Utrecht, Gelderland, Brabant, Limburg) look under-served [ESTIMATE from locations in CMP-04, CMP-17, CMP-67, CMP-75, CMP-76]. Expect 4–9 level-3+ players nationally by 2028 [ASSUMPTION, A-CMP-09]; because ready-mix competes within ~25–50 km (A-CMP-11), regional gaps matter more than national counts.
   > **Note (orchestrator, Phase 7):** this gap is about *recycling* (archetype A). For the *ready-mix* base case (B1), `market.md` §9 ranks Zuid-Holland first on demand density and waterside logistics. The two views do not conflict, but they show a trade-off: Zuid-Holland has the most demand but also everox ('s-Gravendeel) and SL Materials' reach nearby; the under-served centre/south (Brabant) has less recycling competition but Utrecht/Gelderland have grid congestion. The financial model does not depend on the region choice beyond delivery distance and price.
2. **Recycled sand and fines are unused.** Incumbents mostly replace gravel only (Holcim recipes "≤30% as gravel replacement"; Mebin "max 30% of raw materials" view) [VERIFIED, CMP-35, CMP-30]. The 0–4 mm fraction is a cheap feedstock if it can be cleaned (Renewi washing; ARAC separator ~5 kWh/t) [VERIFIED, CMP-67, CMP-86].
3. **Outlet for clean rubble as road-base demand falls.** RAW no longer specifies concrete granulate under roads [VERIFIED, CMP-91]; >90% still goes there [VERIFIED, 2021, CMP-77]. Demolition firms will need high-grade outlets → better gate fees/feedstock terms for a buyer [ASSUMPTION].
4. **Supplier to prefab.** VBI (GreenScore) and Dycore (≥30% RCA) need steady certified RCA and want lower CO₂ [VERIFIED, CMP-47, CMP-51]. A recycler-plus-mixer could sell aggregates and recovered SCM to prefab plants, not only ready-mix.
5. **Project-proof circularity for public clients and mid-size contractors.** Clients want EPD/MKI per project, take-back and traceability (Madaster passports) [VERIFIED, CMP-47, CMP-80]. Regional public coalitions (Betonakkoord tender coalitions; NH Circulaire Deal with 60+ parties) are launching customers [VERIFIED, CMP-81].
6. **Reinforced geopolymer and recovered-cement structural concrete** are not yet commercial at scale (SQAPE is unreinforced first; INVIE not scaled) [VERIFIED, CMP-63, CMP-46]. A first mover with certification could win a niche — but this is a technology risk.
